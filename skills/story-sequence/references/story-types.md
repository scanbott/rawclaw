# Story Sequence Types

These are the available story sequence types. Each has a specific structure and purpose.

Source: Derived from CHRIS STORIES Canva design patterns and Rawgrowth content strategy.

## 1. Authority/Credibility
**Purpose:** Establish expertise and build trust. Lead with proof.
**When to use:** When you have a strong stat, client result, or milestone to share.
**Structure:** Bold claim -> Context -> Proof -> Proof -> Takeaway -> (CTA)
**Example opener:** "I've personally helped generate and save over $520,000 for agencies and consulting businesses."

## 2. Pain Point Agitation
**Purpose:** Make the audience feel the problem they're ignoring. Create urgency.
**When to use:** When targeting a specific pain your audience has. Great for CTA sequences.
**Structure:** Pattern interrupt -> Stack pains -> "You've tried..." -> "But..." -> Real problem -> Answer -> (CTA)
**Example opener:** "Revenue is decent. But you wake up thinking: 'How do I scale this?'"

## 3. Story Arc
**Purpose:** Build connection through personal narrative. Show transformation.
**When to use:** When you have a real experience that illustrates a lesson. Best for engagement.
**Structure:** Scene -> Problem -> Turning point -> Result -> Lesson -> Application -> (CTA)
**Example opener:** "3 years ago I was painting curbs for $50 a pop."

## 4. Education/Value
**Purpose:** Teach something useful. Position as the expert who gives away the playbook.
**When to use:** When you have a framework, insight, or tactic your audience can use immediately.
**Structure:** Contrarian hook -> Framework -> Step 1 -> Step 2 -> Step 3 -> "Now you know" -> (CTA)
**Example opener:** "Most people think AI replaces your team. They're wrong."

## 5. CTA Funnel
**Purpose:** Drive a specific action. Built to convert.
**When to use:** When promoting an offer, lead magnet, booking link, or resource.
**Structure:** Problem hook -> Agitate -> Solution tease -> Proof -> Offer -> CTA with keyword -> (Urgency)
**Example opener:** "You're spending 20 hours a week on content that gets 200 views."
**CTA format:** "DM me '{KEYWORD}' and I'll send you [the thing]"
