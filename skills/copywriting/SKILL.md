# Copywriting Skill

## Before Writing Anything

Load the client's context:
```bash
cat [RAWCLAW]/knowledge/client/business.md
cat [RAWCLAW]/knowledge/client/brand-voice.md
cat [RAWCLAW]/knowledge/client/offer.md
```

The brand voice file overrides everything. If they say never use "AI-powered," never use it. If they say use short sentences, use short sentences.

## The Framework

### Phase 1: Planner
Answer these before writing a word:
- Who is reading this? (be specific -- one person, not a demographic)
- What do they believe right now about this problem?
- What do we want them to do after reading?
- What's the ONE thing they need to understand to take that action?
- What's the format? (email, DM, ad, page, post)
- What's the word count target?

### Phase 2: Generator
Write the copy. Use one of these proven structures:

**PAS (Problem-Agitation-Solution)**
- Problem: Name the pain they recognize
- Agitation: Make it real (consequences, what it costs them)
- Solution: Position the offer as the relief

**AIDA (Attention-Interest-Desire-Action)**
- Attention: Hook that stops the scroll
- Interest: Why this matters to them specifically
- Desire: Paint the outcome they want
- Action: One clear next step

**Before/After/Bridge**
- Before: Their current reality (frustrating)
- After: The outcome they want (vivid)
- Bridge: How we get them there

**Hook formulas:**
- Curiosity gap: "The [topic] mistake 90% of [ICP] make (and how to fix it)"
- Bold claim: "[Specific result] in [specific timeframe] -- here's exactly how"
- Counterintuitive: "Why [common advice] is making your [problem] worse"
- Social proof: "How [type of person] went from [before] to [after]"

### Phase 3: Evaluator
Score the copy 1-10 on each:
- Hook strength: would you stop scrolling? (1-10)
- Clarity: can a 12-year-old follow it? (1-10)
- Specificity: any concrete numbers/outcomes? (1-10)
- Desire: does it make you want the outcome? (1-10)
- CTA: one action, clear, low friction? (1-10)

Average < 7.5 = rewrite. Identify the lowest-scoring element and fix it first.

## Copy Rules

- Lead with their world, not yours
- Specific beats vague (always)
- One idea per piece. One CTA per piece.
- Short sentences. Active voice.
- Never use: "I hope this finds you well", "I wanted to reach out", "game-changing", "revolutionary", "synergy", "leverage" (as a verb)
- Read it aloud before delivering. If it sounds robotic, rewrite.
