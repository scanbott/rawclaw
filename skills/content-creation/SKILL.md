## Hook Library

The hook is everything. If you lose them in the first 3 seconds, nothing else matters.

### Hook Formulas
1. **Curiosity gap:** "The [topic] secret [authority figure] doesn't want you to know"
2. **Counterintuitive:** "Stop doing [common advice]. Here's why it's making things worse."
3. **Specificity:** "I made $[X] in [timeframe] doing [specific thing]. Here's the exact process."
4. **Pattern interrupt:** Start mid-story, mid-conflict -- no setup
5. **Bold claim:** "[Specific result] is possible in [timeframe]. Here's proof."
6. **Fear/urgency:** "If you don't fix [problem] by [timeframe], here's what happens."
7. **Direct address:** "If you're a [specific person] doing [specific thing], watch this."

### Hook Testing Rule
Write 5 hooks before picking one. The first hook is never the best hook.

## Short-Form Script Structure (60-90 seconds)

```
[HOOK - 0-3s]
Single sentence. Creates open loop or pattern interrupt.

[PROBLEM - 3-15s]
Make the pain real. Specific consequences. Numbers if possible.

[INSIGHT - 15-40s]
The thing they didn't know. The mechanism that explains the problem and the solution.

[PROOF - 40-55s]
Quick evidence. Case study, result, screenshot, story.

[CTA - 55-90s]
One action. Not two. "Comment X" or "Follow for more" or "Link in bio." Pick one.
```

## YouTube Script Structure

```
[HOOK - 0:00-0:30]
Open loop: promise the payoff, create curiosity. Don't deliver yet.

[WHY THIS MATTERS - 0:30-1:00]
Why should they care about this specific topic right now?

[CREDIBILITY - 1:00-1:30]
Why you? One sentence, not a paragraph.

[CONTENT BODY]
Deliver the promise. Use pattern: teach → example → teach → example.
Every 2-3 minutes: re-hook ("here's where it gets interesting...")

[RECAP]
Summarize the 3 key things they learned.

[CTA]
Subscribe, watch next video, or take one specific action.
```

## Content Calendar Framework

Weekly structure that works for most businesses:
- Monday: Educational (teach something useful)
- Wednesday: Social proof / case study
- Friday: Polarizing take or personal story
- One wildcard per week: trend, reaction, or behind-the-scenes
