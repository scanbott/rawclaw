## Proposal Structure

1. The situation (what's happening in their world)
2. The problem (what it's costing them)
3. The solution (what you do, not how you do it)
4. The process (what working together looks like)
5. Investment
6. Next step (one clear action)

## Copy Rules

- Lead with their world, not yours
- Specific beats vague (numbers, names, outcomes)
- One CTA per piece
- Short sentences. Active voice.
- Never use: "I hope this finds you well", "I wanted to reach out", "game-changing"
