## Call Funnel Playbook (Jeremy Haynes Frameworks)
Full reference: marketing/research/stolen/jeremy-haynes-playbook.md

### Funnel Structure
- Headline + VSL + integrated Typeform/Calendly. Never separate application and scheduler (50% drop-off).
- Confirmation page: 3 framing videos + loaded testimonials (screenshots from real platforms, 75% of page)
- Only pixel qualified confirmation page. Never pixel the DQ page.
- Use standard events (Schedule, CompleteRegistration), not custom conversions.

### Show Rate System (+30% lift when stacked)
- Every closer gets company iPhone (blue text = real person, green text = spam)
- Selfie video to every booking: "Hey I'm [Name], I saw you answered [specific detail]... shoot me a text back"
- This is the single most effective show rate tactic at scale

#### Pre-Call Touch Sequence (ranked by effectiveness)
1. **Manual SMS within 1 hour of booking (97% effective).** Text from a real number. Offer two pieces of content and let them choose: "Hey [Name], Chris here. I've got two things I can send before we talk -- [resource A] or [resource B]. Which is more useful, text or email? Excited to connect [day/time]." The choice opens a real conversation before the call even starts. Manual gets 85% open rates vs 35% automated.
2. **Selfie video text (82%).** 20-30 second selfie video, one take, no production. Use the "we're all busy" hook, acknowledge their time, name one thing you can help with. Template: "Hey [Name], I know we're all busy, just wanted to put a face to the name before we connect [day]. I think [specific thing] will be really relevant to your situation. Looking forward to it."
3. **Instagram DM as second channel (68%).** If they haven't responded to SMS, find them on Instagram. Spend 2 minutes on their profile. Send a short casual DM, not a pitch, a human intro. Reaching out on a second channel alone shows a 30% lift in contact rate. Template: "Hey [Name], [Your name] here. We have a call coming up and just wanted to connect ahead of it. Looking forward to learning more about what you're building."
4. **PDF proof asset before every call (55%).** Build one proof asset: client results, screenshots, compiled outcomes PDF. Text it before the call as context, not a pitch. One client saw show rates jump from 40% to 67% after adding this step. Template: "Before we talk, wanted to send over some results from recent clients in a similar spot. Not to sell you, just thought it'd be useful context going into our conversation."
5. **Automated SMS 3-touch sequence (35% baseline).** Set up as a floor, not a strategy. (1) Immediate confirmation + one resource, (2) 24 hrs before: reminder + social proof line, (3) 1 hr before: short anticipation-building message. Template: "Hey [Name], confirming our call [day/time]. In the meantime here's [resource link], relevant to what we'll cover. See you then."
6. **Automated email 3-part sequence (18% baseline).** (1) Welcome + proof asset, (2) client story, (3) day-of "what to expect" frame. Every email should deliver standalone value even if the call never happens. Template subject: "Before we talk [Name], here's what to expect." Body: "I put together [resource] based on what you shared, it covers [pain point], which is exactly what we'll dig into."

### Hammer Them (Pre-Call Framing)
- 30-50 short videos across 4 quadrants: Questions, Second-Layer Questions, Objections, Expectations
- 18-email sequence: 6/day for 72 hours. Education, NOT reminders.
- Retarget call bookers with 3-second view exclusions, 15-20 impressions in 72 hours
- Goal: turn salespeople into cashiers

### VSL Framework (7 Steps)
Hook (10-30s) -> Credibility (30-60s) -> Buying Motives (bulk) -> Offer (1-2 min) -> Objections (bulk) -> Qualification (30-60s) -> CTA
- Affluent: 2-15 min, direct, minimal design, conservative claims
- Always state the price. Conditions the pixel for qualified buyers.
- Never use value stacks (not FTC compliant, unbelievable)

### Sales Team Operations
- Dedicated sales manager (not a closer who also manages)
- Daily training on repeat (2-3 week cycle, then restart)
- Hire ahead of demand: 3-6 week hiring lag = lost revenue if reactive
- Disposition data from every lead: financially unqualified, uncertain, trust deficit, timing, spousal, macro-event

### Recruiting Closers (In-House)
- Selfie video as primary filter: Q1 gimme, Q2 "how will you prove honesty?", Q3 real objection
- $50-200/day ads for 1-2 weeks = 1/4 the cost of placement agencies
- Two ideal profiles: young/hungry or family-oriented/stable
- Add friction to recruiting funnels (opposite of lead funnels)

### Scaling Principles
- ROI above 3x: amplify. Don't optimize what works.
- ROI 1.5x-3x: optimize first, then amplify.
- ROI below 1.5x: fix the funnel before spending more.
- Revenue lags action by 1-8 weeks (bullwhip effect). Don't be reactive.
- Sales is the bottleneck, not marketing. Marketing scales overnight.

### Offer Math
- No price complaints = likely underpriced
- Close rates above 80% = probable underpricing
- Charge 10-20% of total value delivered over 12 months
- A/B test new customers only. Grandfather existing customers.

### Client Retention (Anti-Churn)
- 5 churn signals: slower responses, skipped calls, fewer questions, decreased usage, low engagement
- Three-touch re-engagement: empathetic check-in (Day 1), pure value (Day 4-5), direct conversation (Day 10-14)
- Long-term clients = highest risk. Everyone assumes they're fine.

## Hormozi AI Positioning Frames (Stolen from "How to Win With AI in 2026")

### Price Sensitivity Lag (Use on $10K/mo Objections)
The market hasn't repriced yet. Prospects are still paying $5K-$15K/mo in headcount for outputs we deliver at $10K/mo with AI. Our cost to deliver keeps dropping. Their price expectation is stuck. That gap is our margin. Use this when they push back on retainer: "You're probably paying that in salary for one person who does a fraction of this. We deliver it in a system that doesn't call in sick."

### Workflow-Based Audit Frame (Dilan's Discovery Language)
Don't say "we'll automate your team." Say: "For every role you're thinking about hiring, write down the 4-6 things that person actually does with their hands. Then we figure out which of those live in a workflow instead of a salary." Less threatening. More accurate. Gets you in the room.

### BYOS -- Bring Your Own Software
The next wave of high-value employees shows up with their own trained agent stack. Rawgrowth installs that stack at the operator level. We're not selling automation -- we're selling the infrastructure that lets their team operate at 10x without adding headcount.

### Self-Disruption Urgency Close
"One of your competitors is already building an AI-first version of your business. The question isn't whether this happens. It's whether you control it or react to it."

### Revenue Per Employee Proof Point
"We've got clients where revenue per employee is in the millions. Not because they fired everyone -- because they started AI-first and never built the bloat in the first place."

### Train It Like an Employee
When prospects say "AI can't do what humans do" -- reframe: "You wouldn't fire an employee after one bad output. You'd train them. AI works the same way. Give it your rules, your examples, your feedback loop. The difference is 100 training cycles takes a year with a person and 100 minutes with an agent."
