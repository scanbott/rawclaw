## Call Funnel Playbook (Jeremy Haynes Frameworks)
Full reference: ~/knowledge/frameworks/jeremy-haynes-playbook.md

### Funnel Structure
- Headline + VSL + integrated Typeform/Calendly. Never separate application and scheduler (50% drop-off).
- Confirmation page: 3 framing videos + loaded testimonials (screenshots from real platforms, 75% of page)
- Only pixel qualified confirmation page. Never pixel the DQ page.
- Use standard events (Schedule, CompleteRegistration), not custom conversions.

### Show Rate System
- Every closer gets company iPhone (blue text = real person, green text = spam)
- Selfie video to every booking: "Hey I'm [Name], I saw you answered [specific detail]... shoot me a text back"
- This is the single most effective show rate tactic at scale

### Hammer Them (Pre-Call Framing)
- 30-50 short videos across 4 quadrants: Questions, Second-Layer Questions, Objections, Expectations
- 18-email sequence: 6/day for 72 hours. Education, NOT reminders.
- Retarget call bookers with 3-second view exclusions, 15-20 impressions in 72 hours
- Goal: turn salespeople into cashiers

### VSL Framework (7 Steps)
Hook (10-30s) -> Credibility (30-60s) -> Buying Motives (bulk) -> Offer (1-2 min) -> Objections (bulk) -> Qualification (30-60s) -> CTA
- Affluent: 2-15 min, direct, minimal design, conservative claims
- Always state the price. Conditions the pixel for qualified buyers.
- Never use value stacks (not FTC compliant, unbelievable)

### Sales Team Operations
- Dedicated sales manager (not a closer who also manages)
- Daily training on repeat (2-3 week cycle, then restart)
- Hire ahead of demand: 3-6 week hiring lag = lost revenue if reactive
- Disposition data from every lead: financially unqualified, uncertain, trust deficit, timing, spousal, macro-event

### Recruiting Closers (In-House)
- Selfie video as primary filter: Q1 gimme, Q2 "how will you prove honesty?", Q3 real objection
- $50-200/day ads for 1-2 weeks = 1/4 the cost of placement agencies
- Two ideal profiles: young/hungry or family-oriented/stable
- Add friction to recruiting funnels (opposite of lead funnels)

### Scaling Principles
- ROI above 3x: amplify. Don't optimize what works.
- ROI 1.5x-3x: optimize first, then amplify.
- ROI below 1.5x: fix the funnel before spending more.
- Revenue lags action by 1-8 weeks (bullwhip effect). Don't be reactive.
- Sales is the bottleneck, not marketing. Marketing scales overnight.

### Offer Math
- No price complaints = likely underpriced
- Close rates above 80% = probable underpricing
- Charge 10-20% of total value delivered over 12 months
- A/B test new customers only. Grandfather existing customers.

### Client Retention (Anti-Churn)
- 5 churn signals: slower responses, skipped calls, fewer questions, decreased usage, low engagement
- Three-touch re-engagement: empathetic check-in (Day 1), pure value (Day 4-5), direct conversation (Day 10-14)
- Long-term clients = highest risk. Everyone assumes they're fine.
