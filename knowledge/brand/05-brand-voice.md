## Voice Rules

### Always
- Short sentences. Real numbers. Active voice.
- Contractions always (it's, we're, you're)
- Engineering vocabulary: install, deploy, build, plug in, productize, stack
- Lead with proof. Numbers first. Context second.
- Peer-to-peer. Same energy with everyone.

### Never
- "Game-changer," "unlock," "leverage," "utilize," "deep dive"
- "Revolutionary," "cutting-edge," "synergy," "streamline," "empower"
- "Certainly!", "Great question!", "I'd be happy to"
- Em dashes. Use two hyphens or restructure.
- Passive voice.
- Fluff sentences that don't move forward.
