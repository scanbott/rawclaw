# Funnel Architecture Frameworks

Pre-loaded methodology for building conversion funnels. Load this before designing any funnel.

---

## The Core Principle

A funnel's job is to eliminate friction between the moment someone realizes they have a problem and the moment they give you money to solve it.

Every step that doesn't move them closer to a buying decision is a step that kills the conversion.

---

## Funnel Types by Offer

### High-Ticket (>$5K) -- Application Funnel
Best for: consulting, coaching, agency services, done-for-you

```
Traffic (ads / content)
  → VSL or Long-form Sales Page (educates + qualifies)
  → Application Form (pre-qualifies, creates commitment)
  → Discovery / Strategy Call (close on call)
  → Proposal → Onboarding
```

**Key principle:** At high price points, you're not converting on the page. You're getting them to the call. The call closes.

### Mid-Ticket ($500-$5K) -- Webinar Funnel
Best for: courses, group programs, productized services

```
Traffic
  → Registration Page (collect email)
  → Webinar (live or evergreen) -- teach + pitch
  → Replay + Email Sequence (follow-up)
  → Sales Page (for async buyers)
  → Checkout
```

**Key principle:** The webinar is the close. Everything else is getting them to watch and follow up with the ones who didn't buy live.

### Low-Ticket (<$500) -- VSL Funnel
Best for: digital products, courses, small offers

```
Traffic
  → Landing Page with VSL
  → Order Form (with bump offer)
  → Thank You Page (upsell)
  → Email Onboarding
```

**Key principle:** Volume play. Conversion rate matters more than individual close. Optimize relentlessly.

### Lead Generation -- Challenge / Free Value Funnel
Best for: list building, warming cold traffic to high-ticket

```
Traffic
  → Free Offer (lead magnet, challenge, mini-course)
  → Email Nurture Sequence
  → Pivot to Application / VSL / Webinar
```

## VSL Structure

A VSL (Video Sales Letter) is the workhorse of most online funnels.

```
1. HOOK (0:00-0:30)
   Interrupt pattern. Bold claim or counterintuitive statement.
   "What I'm about to show you has helped [specific type of person]
   achieve [specific result] in [specific timeframe]."

2. PROBLEM AGITATION (0:30-2:00)
   Name the pain. Make it real. Amplify consequences.
   Don't rush past this. They need to feel it before they want the solution.

3. THEIR STORY / YOUR AUTHORITY (2:00-4:00)
   Bridge their situation to yours. How did you discover the solution?
   Credibility without arrogance.

4. THE MECHANISM (4:00-7:00)
   Introduce the core idea / approach. Not the product yet -- the mechanism.
   "The reason [common approach] doesn't work is [X]. What works instead is [Y]."

5. THE PRODUCT INTRODUCTION (7:00-10:00)
   Now introduce what you built to deliver the mechanism.
   Features → Benefits → Outcomes.

6. PROOF STACK (10:00-14:00)
   Testimonials, case studies, results. Most specific wins first.

7. THE OFFER (14:00-18:00)
   Everything they get. Make the value tangible. Price anchoring.

8. GUARANTEE (18:00-19:00)
   Remove the risk. Be specific about what you're guaranteeing.

9. THE CLOSE (19:00-20:00)
   Urgency (genuine), restate transformation, single CTA.
```

## Email Sequence Architecture

### Post-Opt-In Nurture (5-7 emails)
```
Day 0: Welcome + deliver the promise (what they signed up for)
Day 1: Your story + social proof
Day 2: Teach something valuable (related to your offer)
Day 3: Address the #1 objection they have
Day 4: Case study email (specific result)
Day 5: The offer introduction
Day 7: Urgency / deadline close
```

### Post-Webinar Follow-Up (4 emails)
```
Day 0 (same day): "You missed the live, here's the replay"
Day 1: Key takeaway from the webinar + FAQ
Day 2: Case study / proof email
Day 3: Final call (replay coming down / cart closing)
```

## Landing Page Structure (High-Converting)

```
ABOVE THE FOLD:
  Headline (transformation promise)
  Subheadline (who it's for + what makes it different)
  CTA button (action-oriented text, not "Submit")
  Social proof bar (logos, numbers, faces)

SECTION 1 -- THE PROBLEM:
  Agitate the situation. Make them feel seen.

SECTION 2 -- THE MECHANISM:
  Introduce the "why" behind your approach.

SECTION 3 -- SOCIAL PROOF:
  Testimonials. Most specific first.

SECTION 4 -- THE OFFER:
  What they get. Bullet points. Benefits not features.

SECTION 5 -- WHO IT'S FOR:
  Qualification. Include AND exclude.

SECTION 6 -- FAQ:
  Address the top 5 objections.

SECTION 7 -- FINAL CTA:
  Restate transformation. Single button.
```

## Conversion Rate Benchmarks

| Funnel Stage | Decent | Good | Excellent |
|--------------|--------|------|-----------|
| Landing page opt-in | 20% | 35% | 50%+ |
| Webinar show rate | 20% | 35% | 50%+ |
| Webinar conversion | 5% | 10% | 20%+ |
| Application completion | 15% | 25% | 40%+ |
| Call show rate | 60% | 75% | 90%+ |
| Call close rate | 15% | 25% | 40%+ |
| VSL → order | 1% | 3% | 8%+ |

If you're below "decent" at any stage, that stage is the constraint. Fix it before optimizing anything else.
