## Framework Reference

Josh Braun's 4T Framework:
- **Trigger**: Why are you reaching out NOW? (timing, event, observation)
- **Think**: What do you think they might be struggling with?
- **Third-Party**: Social proof from someone like them
- **Talk**: Low-friction CTA (not "book a call" -- "worth a conversation?")

Additional principles applied:
- Every email under 75 words (mobile-first)
- No "I" in the first sentence -- lead with THEM
- Subject lines: lowercase, conversational, curiosity-driven
- Zero jargon. Zero buzzwords. Write like a human text message.
- Personalization variables in [BRACKETS]

## Sending Best Practices

**Warm-up protocol**: New domains need 14-day warm-up. Start with 5 emails/day, increase by 5 every 3 days until reaching 50/day per mailbox.

**Infrastructure**:
- Separate sending domain (e.g., mail.rawgrowth.ai or hey.rawgrowth.ai)
- 3-5 mailbox accounts rotating sends
- SPF, DKIM, DMARC all configured
- Smartlead or Instantly for sequencing

**Personalization research (2 min per prospect)**:
- Check their LinkedIn for recent posts/activity (use for [TRIGGER])
- Check company website for team size, services, tech stack
- Check social media for content frequency and type
- Check job postings for hiring signals (hiring for roles AI could replace = strong trigger)

**Response handling**:
- Positive reply: move to setter pipeline, respond within 10 minutes
- "Not now" reply: tag for 30-day re-engagement
- Negative reply: remove immediately, no follow-up
- No reply after sequence: wait 60 days, restart with new angle

**Metrics to track**:
| Metric | Target | Action if Below |
|--------|--------|-----------------|
| Open rate | 50%+ | Fix subject lines |
| Reply rate | 5-10% | Fix body copy / personalization |
| Positive reply rate | 2-5% | Fix targeting / ICP |
| Meeting booked rate | 1-3% of sends | Fix CTA / offer framing |
