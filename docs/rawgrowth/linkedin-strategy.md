## Comment Engagement Strategy

### Where to Comment
- ICP posts about hiring challenges, team scaling, content struggles
- AI industry posts (add nuance, not hype)
- Business growth posts from $1M-$15M founders
- Posts by complementary service providers (coaches, consultants, SaaS founders)

### Comment Framework: V.A.P.
1. **Validate** — Acknowledge the poster's point ("This is exactly right")
2. **Add** — Contribute a specific insight, data point, or contrarian angle
3. **Prompt** — End with a question or observation that invites further discussion

### Example Comments
- On a post about hiring difficulties: "This is the exact pain point that pushed us to build AI departments. One client replaced 4 roles with 8 agents — $150K saved annually, zero turnover. The hiring game is changing faster than most people realize."
- On an AI hype post: "Solid overview but the real gap is implementation. 90% of businesses 'using AI' are just prompting ChatGPT occasionally. The ones winning have trained agents running 24/7 with feedback loops. That's the difference."
- On a content strategy post: "Great framework. Curious — what's your output volume? We've found that AI content engines producing 60+ pieces/week create a compounding effect that manual teams can't match at any budget."

### Rules
- Comment on 10-15 ICP posts per day
- Never pitch in comments — add value, let people come to you
- Always respond to comments on your own posts within 2 hours
- Like and reply to every comment on your posts for the first hour (algorithm signal)

## DM Sequence for Warm Leads

### Trigger: Someone engages with 2+ posts, comments, or connects

**DM 1 — Day 0 (after engagement):**
> Hey [Name] — noticed you've been engaging with some of my AI content. Appreciate that. Quick question: are you currently using any AI automation in your business, or still mostly manual?

**DM 2 — Day 2 (if they respond with interest):**
> Makes sense. Most businesses at your stage are in the same spot — know they need AI but haven't found the right implementation. We install done-for-you AI departments (8 agents, content engine, sales automation, company hub). Would you be open to a 15-minute walkthrough? No pitch — I'll show you what it would look like for [their business type].

**DM 3 — Day 5 (if no response to DM 2):**
> No worries if the timing isn't right. Just wanted to share this quick case study from a [similar business type] we worked with — went from [X] to [Y] in 90 days. [Link or screenshot]. If it's ever relevant, I'm around.

### Rules
- Never send unsolicited pitch DMs
- Always lead with a question, not a statement
- If they say "not interested" — respond with "No problem, appreciate the honesty" and move on
- Maximum 3 DMs in a sequence. Never follow up more than that.
