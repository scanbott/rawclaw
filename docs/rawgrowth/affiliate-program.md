# Raw Growth — Affiliate & Partner Program

## Document Purpose
Formalized partner program structure to create a scalable referral channel. Based on Chris's improvised model ("sell for $25K, keep $5K, we build for $20K") — now systematized.

---

## 1. Program Overview

**Program Name**: Raw Growth Partner Network
**Goal**: Generate 30% of new client revenue through partner-sourced deals within 6 months
**Philosophy**: We pay partners generously because acquisition cost from partners is lower than any paid channel, and the leads are pre-qualified by someone the prospect already trusts.

---

## 2. Three Partner Tiers

### Tier 1: Referral Partner
**For**: Anyone who sends leads our way casually.
- **Commission**: $2,000 per closed deal
- **Requirements**: None. No application needed. Share your unique referral link.
- **Expectations**: No selling required. Just introduce warm contacts.
- **Payment**: Paid within 14 days of client's first payment clearing.
- **Materials Provided**: Referral link, 1-pager PDF, intro email template.
- **Tracking**: Unique link tracks attribution for 90 days.

### Tier 2: Affiliate Partner
**For**: Content creators, coaches, consultants, and operators with an audience who want to actively promote Raw Growth.
- **Commission**: $5,000 per closed deal
- **Requirements**: Application + approval. Must have audience of 1,000+ or network of 20+ qualified contacts.
- **Expectations**: Active promotion (minimum 1 mention per month). Access to training on how to position Raw Growth.
- **Payment**: Paid within 14 days of client's first payment clearing.
- **Materials Provided**: Full marketing kit (see section 7), dedicated partner manager (Dilan), quarterly strategy calls.
- **Tracking**: Unique link + deal registration form.
- **Bonus**: $1,000 extra for every 3 deals closed in a quarter.

### Tier 3: Strategic Partner
**For**: Agencies, SaaS companies, and consultancies that serve the same ICP and want to co-sell AI departments as part of their offering.
- **Commission**: Custom split (typically $5-8K per deal, or 25-30% of setup fee)
- **Requirements**: Application + vetting call with Chris. Must have active client base in our ICP.
- **Expectations**: Joint sales process. Partner brings the lead and participates in discovery call. We handle the build.
- **Payment**: Custom terms per partnership agreement.
- **Materials Provided**: Co-branded materials, joint proposal templates, shared Slack channel, monthly partner meeting.
- **Tracking**: Deal registration + CRM integration.
- **Bonus**: Revenue share on retainer (5% of monthly retainer for the life of the client, capped at 12 months).

## 5. Commission Structure & Payment Terms

### Payment Schedule
| Event | Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Tier 3 |
|-------|--------|--------|--------|
| Client signs contract | - | - | - |
| Client's first payment clears | $2,000 paid | $5,000 paid | Custom amount paid |
| Quarterly bonus (3+ deals) | N/A | +$1,000 | N/A |
| Retainer revenue share | N/A | N/A | 5% of monthly retainer (12 months) |

### Payment Method
- Default: PayPal, Wise, or direct bank transfer
- Minimum payout: $500 (commissions accumulate until threshold)
- Payment net terms: 14 days from client payment clearing
- Tax: Partners are responsible for their own taxes. We issue 1099s (US) or equivalent.

### Commission Rules
- **Attribution window**: 90 days from first touch (link click or deal registration)
- **Multi-touch**: If multiple partners contribute to a deal, commission goes to the first registered referrer
- **Disputes**: Escalate to Chris within 7 days. Decision is final.
- **Clawback**: If a client cancels within 30 days and receives a refund, commission is clawed back.

## 6. Deal Registration Process

### Purpose
Prevent channel conflict. Ensure partners get credit for deals they source.

### Process
1. Partner identifies a qualified lead
2. Partner submits deal registration form: lead name, company, email, estimated revenue, how they know the lead
3. Dilan confirms within 24 hours: (a) lead is new — registered, or (b) lead already in pipeline — declined with explanation
4. Registered deals have 90-day exclusivity. If the lead closes within 90 days, the registering partner gets commission.
5. If 90 days pass with no activity, registration expires. Partner can re-register.

### Deal Registration Form Fields
- Partner name + email
- Lead company name
- Lead contact name + email
- Lead's estimated annual revenue
- How does the partner know this lead?
- Any context about the lead's AI needs
- Preferred introduction method (email intro, DM, warm handoff on a call)

## 9. Legal Considerations

### Simple Partner Agreement (Key Terms)
1. **Parties**: Raw Growth AI LLC and [Partner Name/Company]
2. **Term**: 12 months, auto-renewing unless either party gives 30 days notice
3. **Commission**: Per the tier structure outlined above
4. **Payment**: Net 14 from client payment clearing
5. **Non-exclusivity**: Partners can promote competing products (but if a specific lead is registered, only Raw Growth gets the deal)
6. **Confidentiality**: Partners cannot share client information, pricing details, or internal processes
7. **Representations**: Partners will not make claims about Raw Growth that are not provided in official materials
8. **Termination**: Either party can terminate with 30 days written notice. Outstanding commissions for closed deals will still be paid.
9. **Indemnification**: Each party indemnifies the other for claims arising from their own actions
10. **Governing law**: State of Florida, USA

### What We Need from a Lawyer

- Template Affiliate Partner Agreement (3-4 pages)
- Template Strategic Partner Agreement (custom, 5-7 pages)
- Review of commission clawback provisions
- International partner considerations (UK, EU)
