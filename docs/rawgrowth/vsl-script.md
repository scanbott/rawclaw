# Raw Growth — 15-Minute Video Sales Letter Script

> Full word-for-word VSL script for rawgrowth.ai. Designed for cold traffic from paid ads or organic content.
> Speaker: Chris West, founder. Tone: casual, confident, zero pressure.
> Last updated: 2026-03-29

---

## Technical Notes

- **Target length:** 14-16 minutes
- **Recording style:** Talking head (Chris) + screen recordings + B-roll of dashboards
- **Pacing:** Conversational, not rushed. Let the pauses do work.
- **Music:** Low ambient track underneath. Never competing with voice.
- **Text overlays:** Key numbers and phrases reinforced visually
- **CTA approach:** Soft close. "If this makes sense, here's the next step." No countdown timers, no fake scarcity.

[TEXT OVERLAY: "$10-15K/month → 10-15 posts/week → someone quits → start over"]

[PAUSE]

"Scenario two: You're generating leads — maybe from ads, maybe organic, maybe referrals. They come in. They fill out a form or send a DM. And then... nothing happens. For hours. Sometimes days. You're paying for traffic and then letting it leak through the cracks. Every single day."

[TEXT OVERLAY: "Lead comes in → 6-24 hour response time → lead goes cold"]

[PAUSE]

"Scenario three: You've grown the business to a point where things are starting to break. SOPs live in 40 different Google Docs. Half your processes are in someone's head. When a team member leaves, they take institutional knowledge with them. You're the bottleneck for every decision because nobody else has the full picture. You're doing $3 million but working 70 hours a week. And you're starting to wonder if this thing will ever run without you."

[TEXT OVERLAY: "40 Google Docs. SOPs in people's heads. 70-hour weeks."]

### Editing Style
- Clean jump cuts between sentences (remove pauses, ums, dead air)
- Screen recordings should be smooth, intentional cursor movement
- Text overlays: clean sans-serif, white on dark, emerald accent for key numbers
- Transitions: simple cuts or short fades. No wipes, no effects.
- Lower third for Chris: "Chris West | Founder, Raw Growth" — first appearance only
