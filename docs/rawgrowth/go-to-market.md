# Raw Growth — Go-To-Market Strategy

## Document Purpose
Formalized GTM strategy for scaling Raw Growth from $18K first-client revenue to $100K/month recurring within 6 months.

---

## 1. Current State Analysis

### What's Working
- **Instagram organic → DMs**: Primary lead source. Chris's reels generate warm inbound. 17K DM conversations active.
- **Direct outreach**: 55 warm leads in pipeline from personal network + Instagram engagement.
- **Word of mouth**: Dineline deal ($18K + $10K/mo) came through relationship. Prospects refer other prospects.
- **Demo-first selling**: Showing the product live on calls converts. The AI department is tangible, not theoretical.

### What's Not Working
- **No scalable acquisition channel**: 100% dependent on Chris's personal output. If Chris stops posting, pipeline dries up.
- **No content system**: Reels are improvised, not systematized. No content calendar, no repurposing pipeline.
- **No email list**: Zero owned audience. All leads exist on rented platforms (Instagram, DMs).
- **No SEO presence**: rawgrowth.ai has no organic traffic. Zero blog posts, zero backlinks.
- **No partnership channel**: The improvised affiliate deal with one prospect is not a program.
- **No paid ads**: Not tested. No creative assets, no landing pages, no retargeting.

### Key Insight
Raw Growth is pre-product-market-fit in terms of channel, not product. The product works (Dineline proves it). The challenge is replicable distribution.

**Week 3: Referral Engine**
- Ask Dineline for a testimonial/case study (even preliminary results)
- Create "Refer a friend" offer: existing clients get 1 free month for every referral that closes

**Week 4: Pipeline Acceleration**

- Review Week 1-4 metrics: leads generated, calls booked, close rate

### Phase 2: Add New Channels (Weeks 5-8)

**Week 5: Webinar Prep**
- Build webinar registration page
- Create 3-email confirmation sequence
- Build slide deck and demo flow
- Promote via Instagram, LinkedIn, email list, partner channels

**Week 6: First Webinar + Cold Email Scale**
- Run "AI Department Demo Day" webinar (target: 50 registrants, 20 attendees)

**Week 7: YouTube Launch**

**Week 8: Optimize**
- Review all channel metrics

### Phase 3: Scale Winners (Weeks 9-12)

**Week 9-10: Paid Amplification**
- If Instagram organic is working: boost top-performing reels ($50-100/day)
- If webinar converts: run paid registration ads ($1K test budget)

- Hire first setter (commission-only) to handle DM volume

**Week 11-12: Systematize**
- Document all working playbooks (what to post, what to send, what to say)

- Create onboarding process for new partners
- Project: 10 qualified calls/week by end of month 3

## 7. Revenue Model

### Path to $100K/month
- **Average deal**: $15K setup + $10K/mo retainer
- **Close rate**: 15-20% of qualified calls (current estimate)
- **Calls needed per month for $100K MRR in 6 months**:
  - Need 10 clients on retainer = $100K/mo
  - At 20% close rate, need 50 qualified calls over 6 months
  - That's ~8-9 qualified calls/month
  - At 30% qualification rate from total calls, need ~27-30 total calls/month
  - At 10% booking rate from leads, need 270-300 leads/month

### Funnel Math
```
300 leads/month
  → 30 calls booked (10% booking rate)
    → 9 qualified calls (30% qualification)
      → 2 new clients/month (20% close rate)
        → $30K setup + $20K MRR added per month
```

After 6 months: 10-12 active retainer clients = $100-120K MRR
