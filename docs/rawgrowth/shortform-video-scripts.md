# Raw Growth — Short-Form Video Scripts (Instagram Reels / TikTok)

> 20 hook-first scripts for Chris West. Each optimized for the algorithm: pattern interrupt, value delivery, CTA.
> Last updated: 2026-03-29

---

## Format Guide

- **Platform:** Instagram Reels + TikTok (cross-post)
- **Length:** 15-60 seconds
- **Style:** Talking head + screen recordings. No studio. No polish. Raw and direct.
- **Chris's on-camera energy:** Casual confidence. Leaning back, not forward. Like explaining something obvious to a friend.
- **Text overlays:** Always. 40% of viewers watch without sound.
- **Hook window:** First 3 seconds must stop the scroll. Pattern interrupt or bold claim.

## Production Notes

### General Filming Setup
- **Camera:** iPhone on tripod, eye level, slightly above center
- **Lighting:** Natural light from a window (soft, flattering). Ring light as backup.
- **Background:** Clean, minimal. Dark wall or office setup. Nothing distracting.
- **Framing:** Head and shoulders for talking head. Full desk for BTS/screen recordings.
- **Audio:** Lapel mic or AirPods Pro (noise cancellation). Room tone matters.

### Editing Checklist
- Hook must hit in first 1.5 seconds of the video (not after an intro card)
- Text overlays on every video — sized for mobile (large, high-contrast)
- Captions/subtitles on 100% of videos
- Jump cuts between sentences to maintain pace (no dead air)
- End card with CTA text overlay for 3-5 seconds
- Thumbnail: screenshot the most expressive frame + add bold text overlay

### Posting Strategy
- Post 1 Reel/TikTok per day (minimum 5 per week)
- Best posting times: 8 AM, 12 PM, 6 PM EST
- Cross-post to TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts
- First comment on every post should include the CTA link or keyword prompt
- Respond to every comment in the first hour (algorithm signal)
