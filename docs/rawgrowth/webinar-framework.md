## 4. Live Demo Flow (60-Minute Script Outline)

### Opening (0:00-5:00) — Pattern Interrupt + Credibility
- **0:00-1:00**: "I'm going to show you something that took a restaurant chain from 60 hours of manual work per week to zero. And I'm going to build it live on this call."
- **1:00-3:00**: Quick intro — Chris West, 21, CEO of Raw Growth. Why this exists. The problem: businesses are drowning in manual work and hiring isn't the answer anymore.
- **3:00-5:00**: Agenda overview. "By the end of this 60 minutes, you'll know exactly what an AI department is, what it costs, whether your business qualifies, and you'll have seen one built live."

### Section 1 (5:00-15:00) — The Problem (Pain Amplification)
- **5:00-8:00**: "The Hidden Tax" — calculate the real cost of manual operations in a $3-10M business. Use specific examples: lead follow-up that takes 2 hours/day, reporting that takes 5 hours/week, customer support eating 40 hours/week.
- **8:00-11:00**: "Why ChatGPT and Zapier Aren't Enough" — explain the difference between a tool and a department. Tools do one thing. Departments coordinate, learn, and scale.
- **11:00-15:00**: "The 3 Types of AI Implementations" — (1) DIY tools (cheap, limited, breaks), (2) AI consultants (expensive, theoretical, no execution), (3) AI departments (done-for-you, permanent, scales with you). Position Raw Growth as #3.

### Section 2 (15:00-30:00) — The Solution (Live Demo)
- **15:00-17:00**: "Let me show you what an AI department actually looks like." Share screen. Show the architecture: 8 agents, what each one does, how they connect.
- **17:00-22:00**: **LIVE DEMO — Agent 1**: Customer support AI. Show it handling a real inquiry, responding intelligently, escalating when needed. Real-time.
- **22:00-25:00**: **LIVE DEMO — Agent 2**: Lead follow-up AI. Show it qualifying a lead, sending personalized follow-up, booking a call. Real-time.
- **25:00-28:00**: **LIVE DEMO — Agent 3**: Reporting AI. Show it pulling data from multiple sources, generating a daily briefing, sending it to the CEO. Real-time.
- **28:00-30:00**: "And that's 3 of 8. Each one saves 5-15 hours per week. Combined, you're looking at 40-80 hours per week of human labor replaced."

### Section 3 (30:00-40:00) — The ROI (Make It Undeniable)
- **30:00-33:00**: ROI calculation live. "If you're paying $60K/year for an operations person doing these tasks, and the AI department costs $15K to install + $10K/month... you break even in month 2. By month 6, you've saved $30K net."
- **33:00-36:00**: Dineline case study (or current best case study). Specific numbers. Before and after.
- **36:00-38:00**: "The businesses that will dominate in 2027-2028 are the ones installing AI departments right now. The ones that wait will be competing against AI-powered competitors with a human-only team."
- **38:00-40:00**: Address the elephant in the room: "This is not about replacing your team. This is about giving your team superpowers. The AI handles the repetitive work. Your people handle the creative, strategic, relationship work."

### Section 4 (40:00-45:00) — The Offer
- **40:00-42:00**: "Here's what working with Raw Growth looks like." Walk through the process: Discovery call → Custom scope → 7-90 day installation → Monthly optimization.
- **42:00-43:00**: Pricing: "$15-20K one-time setup, $10K/month optimization retainer. No long-term contracts. Month-to-month. If it's not working, you can leave."
- **43:00-44:00**: "We're taking on [X] new clients this month. If you want to be one of them, book a discovery call. Link is in the chat."
- **44:00-45:00**: Bonus for Demo Day attendees: "If you book a call in the next 48 hours, we'll include a free AI Audit of your business — a $2,500 value. We'll map every process that can be automated and show you the exact ROI."

### Q&A Section (45:00-60:00)
- Dilan feeds questions from chat
- Prioritize questions that let Chris handle common objections naturally
- End with: "Last call — link to book is in the chat. We'll follow up with everyone who registered with the recording and next steps."

## 5. Q&A Handling Guide

### Prepared Answers for Common Questions

| Question | Answer Framework |
|----------|-----------------|
| "How is this different from ChatGPT/Make/Zapier?" | "Those are tools. We build departments. A tool does one task. A department has 8 specialized agents that coordinate, learn from each other, and scale with your business." |
| "Is my data secure?" | "100% — your AI department runs on dedicated hardware (Mac Minis in your infrastructure or ours). Your data never touches shared servers. We sign NDAs with every client." |
| "What if I already have some AI tools?" | "Great — we integrate with what you have. Most clients have 1-2 tools doing 10% of what a full AI department can do. We fill the other 90%." |
| "How long until I see ROI?" | "Most clients see measurable time savings in week 1. Breakeven on investment typically happens by month 2. By month 3, you're in pure profit territory." |
| "What industries do you work with?" | "Online service businesses doing $1-15M/year. Agencies, coaching, SaaS, professional services. If your business runs on information and communication, AI can transform it." |
| "Can we start smaller?" | "Yes — we can start with 3-4 agents and expand from there. The setup fee adjusts based on scope." |
| "I need to talk to my partner first." | "Totally understand. We'll send you a summary document you can share with them, and I'm happy to jump on a quick call with both of you." |

### Questions to Redirect to Discovery Call
- Anything about specific pricing for their business
- Anything about custom implementation details
- Anything that requires more than 2 minutes to answer
- "Can you help with [specific use case]?"

**Redirect script**: "That's a great question and the answer depends on your specific business. That's exactly what the discovery call is for — we'll map your processes and give you a custom scope. Book the call and we'll dive deep into that."

## 8. Attendance Goal Math

```
Target: 2-3 new clients per webinar

Funnel:
  200 registration page visitors (from promotion)
  → 100 registrations (50% conversion rate)
    → 40 live attendees (40% show rate)
      → 8 book discovery calls (20% call booking rate)
        → 6 qualified calls (75% qualification rate)
          → 2-3 close (33-50% close rate from qualified)

Revenue per webinar: 2-3 clients x $15-20K setup + $10K/mo retainer
= $30-60K in setup fees + $20-30K/month in new MRR
```
