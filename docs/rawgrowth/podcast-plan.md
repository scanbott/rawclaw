# Raw Growth — Podcast Launch Plan: "The Raw Growth Show"

## Document Purpose
Complete launch plan for a weekly podcast that builds authority, generates leads, and creates a content engine for all other channels.

---

## 1. Show Concept & Positioning

**Show Name**: The Raw Growth Show
**Tagline**: "How 7-9 Figure Businesses Are Building AI Departments — From the Team Installing Them"
**Host**: Chris West (CEO, Raw Growth)
**Format**: 30 minutes — mix of solo episodes, guest interviews, and behind-the-scenes
**Frequency**: Weekly (every Tuesday)
**Tone**: Direct, no-BS, founder-to-founder. Chris talks like he's in a DM, not on stage.

### Why a Podcast?

1. **Authority**: Hosting a show positions Chris as the expert, not a salesman
2. **Content engine**: 1 podcast episode = 5-10 social media clips + 2-3 LinkedIn posts + 1 email + 1 blog post
3. **Relationship building**: Inviting guests creates warm relationships with potential partners and clients
4. **SEO**: Transcripts become blog posts targeting long-tail keywords
5. **Compounding asset**: Episodes live forever. Episode 1 generates leads in month 12.

### Positioning Differentiation
Most AI podcasts are either: (a) too technical (developers talking to developers) or (b) too hype-y (futurists speculating). The Raw Growth Show is practitioner-focused: we're in the trenches installing AI departments, and we share what actually works, what fails, and what it costs.

## 2. Episode Format

### Standard Episode (30 minutes)

```
0:00 - 0:30    Cold open (most compelling quote or insight from the episode)
```

## 4. Launch Strategy

### Pre-Launch (2 weeks before Episode 1)

**Week -2**:
- Record episodes 1, 2, and 3 (batch recording)
- Set up podcast hosting (Riverside.fm for recording, Buzzsprout for distribution)
- Create cover art and branding (Canva or designer)
- Write show description for all platforms
- Create rawgrowth.ai/podcast landing page

**Week -1**:
- Edit episodes 1-3
- Create trailer (90-second teaser)
- Submit to Spotify, Apple Podcasts, YouTube Podcasts, Amazon Music
- Announce on Instagram, LinkedIn, email list
- DM 50 people personally: "I'm launching a podcast about AI for business. Would mean the world if you subscribed."

### Launch Day (3 Episodes Drop)

**Release episodes 1, 2, and 3 simultaneously**. This gives new listeners multiple episodes to binge (improves algorithm performance).

Launch day plan:
- 6:00 AM: Episodes go live on all platforms
- 8:00 AM: Instagram reel announcement + stories
- 9:00 AM: LinkedIn post with personal story about why the podcast exists
- 10:00 AM: Email blast to full list
- 11:00 AM: DM blast to 100 warm contacts with link
- 12:00 PM: Post in Skool, Facebook groups, Slack communities
- 2:00 PM: Twitter/X thread about Episode 1 key insights
- 4:00 PM: Instagram story poll: "Which episode should I talk about more?"

### Post-Launch (Weekly Cadence)

Every Tuesday:
- New episode goes live
- Instagram reel (best 60-second clip)
- LinkedIn post (key takeaway from episode)
- Email to list (episode summary + link)
- 3-5 short clips scheduled across the week (Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube Shorts)

## 9. Monetization Pathway

### Phase 1 (Months 1-6): Lead Generation (Primary Purpose)
- No direct podcast monetization
- Every episode drives to rawgrowth.ai/demo
- Target: 2-3 discovery calls per month from podcast listeners
- Revenue attribution: track via "How did you hear about us?" on Calendly

### Phase 2 (Months 6-12): Sponsorships (If Audience Justifies)
- Once hitting 500+ downloads per episode
- Seek sponsors from complementary tools (CRMs, project management, analytics)
- Rate: $25-50 CPM (cost per 1,000 downloads)
- At 1,000 downloads/episode: $25-50 per episode in sponsor revenue
- Don't chase this — only accept sponsors that align with audience

### Phase 3 (Months 12+): Premium Content
- Exclusive episodes for clients or community members
- "AI Department Masterclass" series (paid, $97-297)
- Live podcast recordings at events (monetized through event tickets)

### Revenue Math
Primary ROI is not podcast revenue — it's client acquisition:
- 1 client acquired per quarter through podcast influence = $15-20K setup + $10K/mo
- Annual value of 1 podcast-sourced client: $135-140K
- Podcast cost: ~$100/month in tools + 5 hours/week of Chris's time
- ROI: ~100x

## 10. Growth Targets

| Metric | Month 1 | Month 3 | Month 6 | Month 12 |
|--------|---------|---------|---------|----------|
| Downloads per episode | 50-100 | 200-500 | 500-1,000 | 2,000-5,000 |
| Total downloads (cumulative) | 300 | 3,000 | 15,000 | 75,000 |
| Subscribers | 50 | 200 | 500 | 2,000 |
| Reviews (Apple Podcasts) | 5 | 15 | 30 | 75 |
| Clips views (social total) | 5,000 | 25,000 | 100,000 | 500,000 |
| Email subscribers from podcast | 20 | 100 | 300 | 1,000 |
| Discovery calls attributed to podcast | 1 | 3 | 5/mo | 10/mo |

### Growth Tactics
- **First 100 downloads**: Personal outreach. DM everyone you know. Ask for reviews.
- **100-500 downloads**: Guest strategy — appear on other podcasts to drive listeners back
- **500-1,000 downloads**: Paid promotion (boost clips, sponsor newsletter features)
- **1,000+ downloads**: Compound growth from SEO (transcripts), social (clips), and word of mouth

### Success Metric
The podcast is successful when it's generating 3+ qualified discovery calls per month AND providing enough content for the entire social media + email strategy. If it does both, it's the single highest-ROI activity in the marketing stack.
