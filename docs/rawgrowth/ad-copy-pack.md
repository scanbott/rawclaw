## Campaign Setup Notes

### Budget Allocation
| Phase | Daily Budget | Duration | Focus |
|-------|-------------|----------|-------|
| Testing | $50-100/day | 2 weeks | Run all 10 ads, identify top 3 |
| Scaling | $200-500/day | Ongoing | Scale top 3 + A/B variants |
| Retargeting | $30-50/day | Ongoing | Ads 9-10 to website visitors |

### Funnel
1. Ad click → rawgrowth.ai landing page (or dedicated LP)
2. Landing page → book walkthrough (Calendly or Cal.com)
3. Walkthrough → sales call → close

### Key Metrics to Track
- CTR (target: >1.5% for cold, >3% for retargeting)
- CPC (target: <$5 for cold, <$2 for retargeting)
- Cost per walkthrough booked (target: <$100)
- Walkthrough → close rate (target: >15%)
- ROAS at $15-20K initial + $10K/mo ACV

### Creative Testing Protocol
1. Launch all 10 ads with $10-20/day each for 5 days
2. Kill anything with CTR <0.8% after 1,000 impressions
3. Top 3 performers get A/B variants (provided above)
4. Run variants for 7 days at equal budget
5. Winner gets 70% of budget, runner-up gets 30%
6. Refresh creative every 4-6 weeks (audience fatigue)

### Audience Exclusions (All Campaigns)
- Current clients
- People who already booked a walkthrough (last 30 days)
- Employees of Raw Growth
- Under 25 years old (below ICP)
- Countries outside US, UK, Canada, Australia (primary markets)
