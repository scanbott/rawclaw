## How to Use This Document

This is the complete playbook for Raw Growth setters. Every message is copy-paste ready. TextBlaze shortcuts are included for speed -- install the TextBlaze Chrome extension, then type any `/rg-` command to instantly paste the corresponding message.

**Variables you need to swap before sending:**
- `[FIRST NAME]` -- the prospect's first name
- `[BUSINESS TYPE]` -- what they do (agency, coaching biz, SaaS, etc.)
- `[PAIN POINT]` -- the specific problem they mentioned
- `[CASE STUDY]` -- a relevant example or result you can reference
- `[NUMBER]` -- any relevant metric (revenue, team size, content volume)
- `[LEAD SOURCE]` -- how they found Raw Growth (website, referral, ad, social, etc.)

**Rules:**
1. Never send more than 3 sentences in a single DM bubble
2. Use voice notes when you can -- they convert 2-3x better than text
3. Never quote price in DMs. Ever. Move to call
4. Priority order every morning: closest to cash first (post-call follow-ups > booked nurture > warm convos > new inbounds > cold outbound)
5. Match their energy. If they send one word, you send one line. If they send a paragraph, match it

## The Pipeline

```
Lead Source --> New Convo --> Pain Identified --> Call Proposed --> Call Booked --> Call Showed --> Qualified --> Closed
```

### Conversion Benchmarks

| Stage | Target Rate | Notes |
|-------|-------------|-------|
| Lead --> New Conversation | 30-40% | From inbound; 5-10% from cold outbound |
| New Convo --> Pain Identified | 60-70% | Should happen within 4-6 messages |
| Pain Identified --> Call Proposed | 80%+ | If pain is real, always pitch the call |
| Call Proposed --> Call Booked | 40-50% | This is the hardest conversion. Scripts matter |
| Call Booked --> Call Showed | 80%+ | Pre-call nurture sequence is critical |
| Call Showed --> Qualified | 70-80% | Closer qualifies on-call |
| Qualified --> Closed | 25-35% | Closer handles; average deal $15-20K setup + $10K/mo |

### Revenue Math

At $15K average setup + $10K/mo retainer:
- 3 closes/month = $45K setup + $30K MRR = $75K/month
- 5 closes/month = $75K setup + $50K MRR = $125K/month
- That requires ~15-25 showed calls/month
- That requires ~20-30 booked calls/month
- That requires ~50-75 call pitches/month
- That requires 150-250 active conversations/month

## Section 1: Inbound DM Scripts

When someone reaches out -- through a website form, a reply to an ad, a DM after seeing content, a referral, or any other lead source -- the clock starts. Respond within 2 minutes during business hours.

### 1A. Immediate Response (Within 2 Minutes)

These are your first messages after someone reaches out or shows interest. Goal: acknowledge, be human, start a conversation.

---

**Script 1A-1: The Casual Acknowledger**
`/rg-inbound1`

> hey [FIRST NAME]! saw your message -- appreciate you reaching out

> quick question before anything else -- what kind of business are you running?

---

**Script 1A-2: The Energy Matcher**
`/rg-inbound2`

> yo what's good [FIRST NAME] -- glad you hit us up

> just curious, what caught your attention about Raw Growth? always like hearing what resonated

---

**Script 1A-3: The Direct Opener**
`/rg-inbound3`

> [FIRST NAME]! appreciate the interest. let me make sure I point you in the right direction

> are you running a [BUSINESS TYPE] or something else? just want to understand your world before I jump in

---

### 1B. Deliver Value + Transition to Qualifying

After they reply to your opener, give them something useful (a relevant resource, insight, or example) and immediately pivot to qualifying.

---

**Script 1B-1: Value Delivery + Pivot**
`/rg-deliver`

> appreciate the context. here's a quick breakdown of what we've been doing for [BUSINESS TYPE] companies -- [link to relevant resource or example]

> the businesses that get the most out of what we build are usually doing $50K+/month and are stuck doing everything manually or burning through team members trying to scale content

> does any of that sound like your situation right now?

---

**Script 1B-2: Value Delivery + Specific Hook**
`/rg-deliver2`

> let me send you something that's relevant to your situation -- [link]

> random question -- how are you handling content and marketing right now? doing it yourself or do you have a team on it?

---

### 1C. Qualification Questions (Gap Framework)

These questions dig into their current situation, desired situation, and the gap between them. Ask them one at a time -- don't dump all four at once.

---

**Question 1: What They Do**
`/rg-qualify1`

> nice -- so what does your business look like right now? what do you sell and roughly how big is the team?

---

**Question 2: Current Revenue / Scale**
`/rg-qualify2`

> that's solid. roughly what range are you at revenue-wise? don't need exact numbers -- just trying to understand the stage you're at

---

**Question 3: What They've Tried**
`/rg-qualify3`

> have you messed with any AI tools or tried to automate any of the content/marketing side? curious what you've tested

---

**Question 4: What's Not Working**
`/rg-qualify4`

> got it. so if you could wave a magic wand and fix one thing in the business right now -- the thing that's burning the most time or money -- what would it be?

---

### 1D. Transition to Call Pitch

Once pain is identified, pitch the call. Frame it as a fit check, not a sales pitch.

---

`/rg-pitch`

> honestly [FIRST NAME], what you're describing is exactly what we built the AI department to solve

> would it make sense to jump on a quick call so I can show you exactly how it would look for [BUSINESS TYPE]? not a sales pitch -- just want to see if there's a fit and show you the dashboard

**Script 1D-2: The Curiosity Pitch**
`/rg-pitch2`

> so here's the thing -- what you're doing manually right now, we've got AI agents that handle that on autopilot. like, 60+ pieces of content a week, sales call analysis feeding back into your copy, the whole thing

> I could try to explain it all in DMs but honestly it makes way more sense to just show you. got 20 minutes this week?

`/rg-pitch3`

## Section 6: Objection Handling Library

Every objection follows the same framework:
1. **Acknowledge** -- validate their concern, never dismiss
2. **Reframe** -- shift the perspective to reveal a different truth
3. **Close** -- move them forward with a question or micro-commitment

## Section 8: Daily Setter Workflow

### Hour-by-Hour Execution Plan

**Priority Hierarchy (follow this order every single time):**
1. Closest to cash (post-call follow-ups, people who said "let me think about it")
2. Booked calls (pre-call nurture -- confirmations, reminders)
3. Active warm conversations (people mid-qualification)
4. New inbounds (comment triggers from the last 12 hours)
5. No-show recovery
6. Dead lead revival
7. Cold outbound prospecting

---

**8:00 AM -- Check Overnight DMs (30 min)**
- Open all unread DMs
- Respond to every inbound that came in overnight
- Use `/rg-inbound1`, `/rg-inbound2`, or `/rg-inbound3` depending on context
- Tag each new convo in your tracking sheet: Name, Business, Stage, Date

**8:30 AM -- Follow Up on Yesterday's Conversations (30 min)**
- Go through every open conversation from yesterday
- Anyone who was mid-qualification: send next qualifying question
- Anyone who went quiet: send a casual bump
- Anyone who was pitched but didn't book: send a "just checking in" message
- Priority: people closest to booking > people mid-qualification > people who just started

**9:00 AM -- Pre-Call Nurture (15 min)**
- Check who has calls booked today and tomorrow
- Send `/rg-pre24` to anyone with a call tomorrow
- Send `/rg-pre2h` to anyone with a call in 2 hours
- If anyone has a call in 15 min, set a timer and send `/rg-pre15m`

**9:15 AM -- Post-Call Follow-Up (15 min)**
- Check with the closer: did yesterday's calls result in any action items?
- If someone needs a proposal sent -- coordinate with the closer
- If someone needs to be re-booked -- reach out with a new time
- If someone closed -- update tracking and celebrate

**9:30 AM -- Outbound Prospecting Block 1 (90 min)**
- Open your prospecting channels (Instagram, LinkedIn, X, Skool, etc.)
- Find 20-30 new prospects using:
  - Competitor accounts' followers and commenters
  - Industry-relevant hashtag searches (#agencyowner, #scaleyourbusiness, #aitools, #businessautomation)
  - LinkedIn searches for [BUSINESS TYPE] owners
  - Skool communities and Facebook groups in target niches
  - Referral requests from existing network
- Send personalized cold DMs using `/rg-cold1` through `/rg-cold10`
- Target: 20-30 new conversations opened per session
- Log every outreach in tracking sheet

**11:00 AM -- Respond to Morning Replies (30 min)**
- By now, some of your morning messages will have replies
- Continue conversations, advance people through qualification
- Book anyone who's ready
- Note: this is often your highest-conversion window

**11:30 AM -- Lunch / Break (30 min)**

**12:00 PM -- No-Show Recovery (15 min)**
- Check if any calls from today were no-shows
- Send `/rg-noshow1` immediately
- Check yesterday's no-shows and send `/rg-noshow2`

**12:15 PM -- Dead Lead Revival (30 min)**
- Go through leads that went cold 30+ days ago
- Send 10-15 revival messages using `/rg-revive1`, `/rg-revive2`, `/rg-revive3`
- Log results -- these have a surprisingly good conversion rate when done right

**12:45 PM -- Mid-Day Conversation Management (45 min)**
- Work through all active conversations
- Focus on moving people from "pain identified" to "call pitched"
- Use pain discovery questions and agitation/future-pacing scripts
- Book anyone who's ready

**1:30 PM -- Outbound Prospecting Block 2 (60 min)**
- Open a fresh batch of 15-20 prospects
- Mix channels: Instagram DMs, LinkedIn messages, Twitter/X DMs
- Use different cold openers than the morning block
- Target: 15-20 additional new conversations

**2:30 PM -- Afternoon Reply Management (60 min)**
- This is your second wave of replies
- Continue qualification conversations
- Handle any objections that come in
- Book calls

**3:30 PM -- End-of-Day Wrap-Up (30 min)**
- Update your tracking sheet: total new convos, total pitches, total booked
- Flag any hot leads for tomorrow morning follow-up
- Prepare your tomorrow morning follow-up list
- Send any final replies

**4:00 PM -- EOD Report to Team Lead**
- Submit daily numbers:
  - New conversations opened
  - Conversations advanced to qualification
  - Calls pitched
  - Calls booked
  - No-shows recovered
  - Revenue of booked calls (if tracking pipeline value)

## Section 13: Setter Do's and Don'ts

### DO:

1. **Respond within 2 minutes** to inbound DMs during business hours
2. **Use their name** in every message
3. **Ask questions** -- let them talk more than you
4. **Reference specific details** they've shared (shows you're listening)
5. **Send voice notes** when the conversation gets deeper
6. **Follow up at least 8 times** before marking someone dead
7. **Keep messages short** -- 1-3 sentences max per bubble
8. **Track everything** -- every conversation, every stage change, every result
9. **Study Raw Growth's content** -- you need to speak the brand's language
10. **Celebrate wins** with the team -- booked calls, closes, funny DM moments

### DON'T:

1. **Never quote price in DMs** -- always move to a call
2. **Never pitch before qualifying** -- you need to know their pain first
3. **Never send more than 3 follow-ups in one day** to the same person
4. **Never badmouth competitors** -- reframe instead of attacking
5. **Never use corporate language** -- "synergies", "leverage", "optimize" are banned
6. **Never send a message that could come from a bot** -- every message should feel human
7. **Never give strategic advice** -- that's what the call is for. DMs are for diagnosing, not prescribing
8. **Never lie about results** -- if we don't have a case study for their exact industry, say "we're still building cases in [INDUSTRY] but here's what we've done in similar businesses"
9. **Never argue with a prospect** -- if they push back, acknowledge and reframe
10. **Never let a hot lead sit overnight** -- if someone is ready to book, book them NOW

## Appendix B: Lead Source Response Guide

Different lead sources require slightly different first-touch energy. Here's how to adjust:

| Lead Source | Temperature | First Touch Style |
|-------------|-------------|-------------------|
| Website form / booking request | Hot | Skip rapport, go straight to qualifying. They came to you |
| Referral from existing client | Hot | Name-drop the referrer immediately. Trust is pre-built |
| Replied to paid ad | Warm | They saw an offer -- acknowledge what caught their eye, qualify fast |
| Social media DM (organic) | Warm | They've seen content -- ask what resonated, then qualify |
| Cold outbound from you | Cold | Lead with personalization and a question. Earn the right to qualify |
| Skool / Facebook group find | Cold-Warm | Reference the shared community. Build micro-rapport first |
| LinkedIn connection | Cold | Be professional but casual. More business context, less "bro" energy |

For ALL lead sources, the flow is the same once you're in conversation:
1. Acknowledge and respond fast (under 2 minutes for inbound)
2. Ask a qualifying question
3. Deliver relevant value (resource, example, insight)
4. Continue qualification (Gap Framework)
5. Pitch the call
