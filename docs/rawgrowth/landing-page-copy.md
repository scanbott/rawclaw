## Page-Level Design Notes

**Overall tone**: Confident, direct, no fluff. Reads like a smart friend explaining something valuable, not a corporation selling software.

**Typography**: Large, bold headlines. Short paragraphs. Generous white space. Easy to scan on mobile.

**Color palette**: Dark background (near-black or deep navy). White text. Accent color for CTAs (electric blue or emerald green). Minimal color -- let the copy do the work.

**Imagery**: Dashboard screenshots (real, not mockups). No stock photos of people pointing at screens. If showing people, use real team photos or nothing.

**Mobile**: Every section must work on mobile first. CTA buttons full-width on mobile. FAQ as accordion.

**Page speed**: Under 2 seconds load time. No video autoplay above the fold. Lazy-load everything below the fold.

**Tracking**: UTM parameters on all inbound links. Form submissions tracked as conversions in Google Analytics + Meta Pixel. Hotjar or Clarity for scroll depth and heatmaps.
