# Raw Growth — Customer Retention Playbook

## Document Purpose
Keep clients on retainer beyond month 3 and expand revenue per client over time. The goal: zero voluntary churn in the first 6 months, and 90%+ retention at 12 months.

---

## 1. Retention Philosophy

The #1 reason B2B clients churn is not that the product stops working. It's that they stop seeing value. Every month, the client must be able to answer: "What did my AI department do for me this month that's worth $10,000?"

Our job is to make that answer obvious, automatic, and impossible to ignore.

## 3. Monthly Business Review Template

### Meeting Structure (60 minutes)

**0-5 min: Warm-up**
- How's the business going overall? Any big changes?
- Any team feedback on the AI department?

**5-20 min: Performance Report**
Present the Win Report (see section 4):
- Tasks completed by each agent this month
- Hours saved (translate to dollar value)
- Error rate and accuracy metrics
- Uptime and reliability stats
- Month-over-month trends

**20-30 min: ROI Discussion**
- "Here's what you invested this month: $10K retainer"
- "Here's what your AI department produced: [specific value]"
- "Net ROI this month: [number]"
- Compare to what the equivalent human labor would cost

**30-40 min: What's Next**
- Agent improvements planned for next month
- New capabilities available
- Process changes on the client's side that affect the AI department
- Expansion opportunities (see section 5)

**40-50 min: Feedback & Issues**
- Anything not working as expected?
- Any new pain points the AI department could address?
- Team adoption feedback — are people using the agents?

**50-60 min: Action Items**
- Summarize commitments for next month
- Set date for next review
- Any escalations needed

### Post-Meeting Deliverable
Within 24 hours, Dilan sends:
- Meeting summary email
- Updated performance dashboard
- Action items with owners and deadlines
- Next meeting calendar invite

## 6. Churn Risk Signals & Intervention Playbook

### Early Warning System

| Signal | Risk Level | Detection Method | Intervention |
|--------|-----------|-----------------|--------------|
| Client stops responding to check-in emails | Medium | Dilan monitors response times | Chris sends personal video message |
| Agent usage drops > 30% week-over-week | High | Automated monitoring | Same-day call: "We noticed usage dropped. What changed?" |
| Client mentions budget tightening | High | Call notes / Slack | Proactive ROI report + "here's how to get more value" |
| Client team not using agents (adoption failure) | Critical | Usage metrics | Offer free team training session |
| Client asks about contract terms or cancellation | Critical | Chris/Dilan flag | Chris calls within 2 hours. Discovery: what's wrong? |
| Monthly review gets rescheduled 2+ times | Medium | Calendar tracking | Dilan sends async video update instead |
| Client hires an "AI person" internally | High | Relationship intel | Position as complementary: "Your AI person + our department" |
| Client mentions a competitor by name | Medium | Call notes | Battlecard response + differentiation conversation |
| Payment fails or is late | High | Billing system | Dilan reaches out warmly, not punitively |
| Client's business contracts (layoffs, revenue drop) | High | Relationship intel | Offer reduced scope at reduced rate (keep them, don't lose them) |

### Intervention Escalation Path
1. **Level 1** (Dilan): Check-in message, async video update, minor issue resolution
2. **Level 2** (Chris): Personal call, ROI deep-dive, relationship repair
3. **Level 3** (Chris + Alexander): Emergency optimization sprint, custom solution, pricing adjustment
4. **Level 4** (Chris): Save conversation — honest discussion about what would need to change

### Save Offers (Last Resort)
- **Reduced scope**: Drop to 4 agents at $6K/month (better than losing $10K/month)
- **Payment plan**: Defer 1 month's payment, spread over next 3 months
- **Performance guarantee**: "Give us 60 days. If we don't hit [metric], next month is free"
- **Pause option**: 1-month pause (hold their setup, resume when ready) — better than cancellation

## 7. Client Health Score Formula

### Scoring Model (0-100)

| Factor | Weight | Metric | Scoring |
|--------|--------|--------|---------|
| **Agent Usage** | 25% | % of available agent capacity utilized | 90%+ = 25, 70-89% = 20, 50-69% = 15, <50% = 5 |
| **Engagement** | 25% | Attendance at reviews + response time to messages | All reviews attended + <24hr responses = 25, Misses 1+ review = 15, Unresponsive = 5 |
| **Results** | 30% | Documented ROI vs. investment | ROI > 3x = 30, ROI 2-3x = 25, ROI 1-2x = 15, ROI < 1x = 5 |
| **Satisfaction** | 20% | NPS or direct feedback | Promoter (9-10) = 20, Passive (7-8) = 15, Detractor (1-6) = 5 |

### Health Categories
| Score | Status | Action |
|-------|--------|--------|
| 80-100 | Healthy | Expansion conversation, case study request, referral ask |
| 60-79 | Watch | Increase touch frequency, proactive optimization |
| 40-59 | At Risk | Chris intervention call, root cause analysis, save plan |
| 0-39 | Critical | All hands: emergency optimization, pricing adjustment, save offers |

### Tracking
- Calculate monthly after each business review
- Trend over time (improving, stable, declining)
- Flag any client that drops 15+ points in a single month

## 8. Quarterly Strategy Sessions

### Purpose
Go beyond operational updates. Discuss the client's business strategy and how AI can support their next phase of growth.

### Agenda (90 minutes, every 3 months)
1. **Business Update (15 min)**: Where is the business heading? New goals, challenges, market changes?
2. **AI Department Retrospective (20 min)**: What worked, what didn't, key learnings from the quarter
3. **Industry Trends (10 min)**: New AI capabilities relevant to their business (we bring insights)
4. **Strategic Roadmap (25 min)**: Next quarter's priorities. What should the AI department focus on?
5. **Expansion Discussion (10 min)**: New agents, new capabilities, new integrations
6. **Relationship Check (10 min)**: How's the partnership working? Any changes needed on our side?

### Deliverable
Quarterly Strategy Brief (1-2 pages): key decisions, roadmap, action items. Sent within 48 hours.

## 9. Client Community Best Practices

### Channel Setup (Slack or Discord)
- **#general**: Announcements, updates from Raw Growth
- **#wins**: Clients share their AI department wins (peer motivation)
- **#support**: Technical questions, quick fixes
- **#feature-requests**: Ideas for new agents or capabilities

- Clients only (no prospects, no partners — this is exclusive)
- No selling to each other (unless coordinated through Raw Growth)
- Raw Growth team responds within 4 hours during business hours
- Monthly "Community Spotlight" — feature one client's success story

### Community Value
- Peer learning (clients teach each other)
- Social proof reinforcement (seeing others succeed reduces churn)
- Feature request aggregation (build what multiple clients want)
- Organic referrals (clients meet each other and refer friends)

## 11. Case Study Extraction Process

### When to Extract
- Client health score > 80 for 2+ consecutive months
- Client has specific, quantifiable results
- Client has verbally expressed satisfaction
- Ideally at or after the 90-day mark

### Extraction Process

**Step 1: Ask Permission**
```
"[Name], your AI department has been crushing it — [specific metric].
Would you be open to us creating a short case study about your results?
We'd share the draft with you for approval before publishing anything."
```

**Step 2: Interview (30 min)**
Questions:
1. What was your business like before the AI department? What were the biggest pain points?
2. What made you decide to invest in Raw Growth specifically?
3. Walk me through the implementation — what was that like?
4. What results have you seen? (Push for specific numbers)
5. What surprised you most about having an AI department?
6. What would you say to someone who's considering this but hasn't pulled the trigger?
7. Any specific agent or capability that's been a game-changer?

**Step 3: Draft Case Study**
Format:
- Challenge (2-3 paragraphs)
- Solution (what we built, timeline)
- Results (specific metrics, before/after)
- Client quote (pull from interview)
- Visual: before/after process diagram

**Step 4: Client Approval**
Send draft, get sign-off. Offer to let them review for accuracy and tone.

**Step 5: Publish**
- Website case study page
- PDF version for sales team
- Social media clips (quotes + metrics)
- Email to prospects (nurture sequence)
- Partner marketing kit
